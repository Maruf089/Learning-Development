# What is ERP?

**ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)** is software that integrates all core business processes into one system.

> Think of it as a central control for a company.

## Why is ERP Important?

ERP helps businesses manage and automate their processes for efficiency and better decision-making.

### Common ERP Modules

- Finance & Accounting
- HRM (Human Resource Management)
- Inventory
- Sales & Purchase
- Payroll

**Popular ERP Systems:** SAP, Oracle ERP, Microsoft Dynamics, Odoo


📊 What is CRM?
CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is software that helps businesses manage interactions with customers.

📘 Core Accounting Concepts (Very Important for ERP)
1. Journal (Journal Entry)
A Journal is the first place where transactions are recorded.
👉 Every transaction has:
Debit (Dr)
Credit (Cr)

👉 In ERP:
Each transaction creates a journal entry automatically

2. Ledger
A Ledger is a collection of accounts that shows the financial transactions of a company.
👉 In ERP:
Each account in the ledger is updated automatically with the journal entries.

💡 Think:

Journal = chronological record
Ledger = categorized record

3. Chart of Accounts (COA)

A structured list of all accounts in a company.

👉 Examples:

Inventory
Sales Revenue

4. Debit & Credit (Golden Rule)

The Golden Rule of Accounting:
- Debit what comes in
- Credit what goes out  
👉 In ERP:
When you buy inventory, you debit Inventory and credit Cash.
When you make a sale, you debit Cash and credit Sales Revenue.


5. Trial Balance

A report that checks:
👉 Total Debit = Total Credit

If not equal → error exists.

👉 In ERP:

Used for validation before reports are generated.


6. Balance Sheet

Shows company’s financial position:

👉 Formula:
Assets = Liabilities + Equity

7. Income Statement (Profit & Loss)
Shows company’s performance over a period:
👉 Formula:
Revenue - Expenses = Net Income

8. Accounts Payable (AP)

Money the company owes to suppliers

👉 Example:

You bought goods but haven’t paid yet

9. Accounts Receivable (AR)
Money the company is owed by customers
👉 Example:
You made a sale but haven’t received payment yet

10. Inventory Management
Tracking and managing stock levels, orders, sales, and deliveries.
👉 In ERP:
Automatically updates inventory levels with each transaction.

11. Payroll
Managing employee compensation, benefits, and taxes.
👉 In ERP:
Automates salary calculations, tax deductions, and payslips.
